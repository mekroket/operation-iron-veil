# Art Direction

The look is blue hour on an overcast industrial port: cold sky and sea, haze that eats the distance, and warm sodium pools on wet concrete. Time of day is 04:42, and a dim warm band on the eastern horizon says dawn is close. Everything is generated by code at load time.

Sources: `src/scene/skyPaint.ts`, `SceneEnvironment.ts`, `sunShadows.ts`, `GraphicsManager.ts`, `src/levels/render/LightRig.ts`, `materialLibrary.ts`, `props/propTypes.ts`.

## Materials

Each surface is one cached `PBRMaterial` with a procedural albedo, tangent-space normal and ORM map (R occlusion, G roughness, B metal). Generators are in `texMineral.ts`, `texMetal.ts` and `texInterior.ts`.

- **Level surfaces (`MaterialKey`):** asphalt, concrete, concreteDark, concretePainted, quayStone, gravel, steelDark, steelRust, steelYellow, corrugatedGrey, corrugatedRust, corrugatedWhite, plasterInterior, floorTile, floorRubber, woodPlank, glassDark, hazardStripe. Each maps to a physics surface in `MATERIAL_SURFACE`, which drives footsteps and impacts.
- **Prop-only surfaces:** paintWorn, metalBare, rubber, canvas, chainLink.
- **Special cases:** `glassDark` is alpha blended at 0.62. `chainLink` is alpha tested, double-sided, and does not cast shadows.
- **Self-lit:** `glowSodium`, `glowWhite`, `glowRed` and `glowAmber` are emissive, push past the bloom threshold, and are frozen. `led` and `screen` are emissive with animated or recoloured textures.
- **Decals:** one atlas holds container marks (HALCYON LINE, NORTHMARK, TRANSMERE, CASTELLAN), warning signs and vehicle marks. All names are invented.
- **Level UVs** are world-space at 3 m per repeat, so nothing stretches and neighbours line up. Tall walls get a darker damp foot band through vertex shade, and each solid gets a slight tone shift so long runs do not read as one extrusion.
- **Characters and guns** use small `PBRMaterial` sets with flat albedo. Guns are coloured by vertex tint so that each material stays one shader and one bind.

## Lighting rules

1. **Base lighting is cold and weak.** A directional moon and dawn light from the east-south-east (intensity 1.35, colour 0.7, 0.76, 0.92), a hemispheric ambient (0.42, sky 0.42, 0.52, 0.72, ground 0.1, 0.07, 0.05), and an environment cube at intensity 1.5 so metals stay readable.
2. **One function paints sky and reflections.** The dome and the environment cube both come from `sampleSky`, so reflections always agree with what is visible. Do not add a separate reflection source.
3. **Warmth comes from practical lights only.** Do not warm the moon, the ambient or the fog.
4. **Every mesh has a light budget.** The sun and sky take two slots of `gfx.maxLights`, and the rest go to the highest-scoring fixtures through `includedOnlyMeshes`. Indoor kinds get a score bonus. Moving meshes re-pick their lights every 0.5 s.
5. **Lights end at their range.** `FALLOFF_GLTF` fades to zero at range, and intensity scales with range squared so brightness at the edge is similar across kinds.
6. **Never leave `includedOnlyMeshes` empty.** Empty means every mesh to Babylon, so an unused light must be disabled instead.
7. **Fake what the budget cannot pay for.** `LightFx` adds additive flares, haze cones under lamps and, at 4 or fewer lights, painted floor pools.
8. **Shadows belong to the moon only.** One render-once PCF shadow map follows the camera in texel-snapped steps. Self-lit, printed and see-through surfaces never cast (`NON_CASTING`). Sun shadow darkness is 0, so shadowed areas receive no moonlight.

## Atmosphere and post

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Fog | EXP2, density 0.0065, colour equals the horizon so distant geometry dissolves into the sky |
| Silhouettes | Far hills and a gantried breakwater, hand-faded and fog-free |
| Sea | Tiled animated normals, snapped to the swell tile |
| Tone map | ACES, exposure 1.55, contrast 1.12 |
| Colour curves | Shadows hue 195 (teal), density 34, saturation 12. Highlights hue 34 (warm), density 26, saturation 8 |
| Vignette | Multiply, weight 1.3, stretch 0.35, cold near-black |
| Dithering | On, intensity 1/255, because dark gradients band on 8-bit displays |
| Bloom | Threshold 0.95, weight 0.32, kernel 64, scale 0.5. Lit surfaces stay under the threshold, while emissives, lamps and flashes exceed it |
| Grain | Intensity 5, animated, High and Ultra only |
| SSAO | Ultra only, see `PERFORMANCE.md` |

Sharpen, chromatic aberration and depth of field stay off.

## Silhouette and characters

**Black Meridian contractors** (`EnemyRigAssets.ts`): charcoal fatigues, grey plate carrier, dark webbing and pouches, helmet with side rails and ear cups, black balaclava with a skin eye strip, and a burnt-orange upper-arm identifier. That orange is the only saturated colour on the body, so it reads through haze. Built from instanced primitives.

**Sgt. Calloway** appears only as first-person arms (`gunArms.ts`): olive sleeves with fold details, dark cuffs, near-black gloves with lighter knuckles. No full body model exists.

**Weapons** (`gunModels.ts`): VR-7 WARDEN has a tan handguard and stock, gunmetal receiver and red-dot reflex sight. SX-4 HORNET is a square black body with orange base pad and foregrip cap, wire stock and a green sight dot. PX-2 MARSHAL is a gunmetal slide on a black frame with three sight dots.

## Do and do not

- Keep the sky dim and the fog at the horizon colour, so the two always meet.
- Reserve dirty white containers for the Veil story. In the yard, Block B south is the deliberate exception to mixed colours.
- Use invented names only. No real brands, insignia, flags or firearm makers.
- Do not place a light without checking the per-mesh budget at the Low and Medium presets.
- Keep every prop inside its `PROP_CATALOG` box, so that what you see is what you collide with.
