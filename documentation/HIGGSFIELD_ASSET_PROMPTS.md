# Higgsfield Asset Prompts

Copy-paste prompts for concept art and promotional media for OPERATION: IRON VEIL. Colours, uniforms and buildings are taken from the game's code, so generated art matches what the player sees.

## WARNING: this is concept art, not game assets

> **Generated images and video are concept art. They are not game-ready 3D models.**
>
> - A flat image must never be passed off as 3D geometry. Do not map a picture of a container onto a plane, billboard or box and present it as a modelled prop or character.
> - An image has no topology, UVs, PBR maps, rig, collision or correct scale. Nothing here can enter the 3D world without the process in `ASSET_PIPELINE.md`.
> - Legitimate direct uses are flat 2D places only: menu backgrounds, briefing art, loading screens, trailers and store pages.
> - Say plainly in any public material when an image is concept art and not in-game footage.

Check that your plan's terms allow your intended use, and record each kept output in the asset register described in `ASSET_PIPELINE.md`.

## How to use

1. Paste **Style Block** first, then any character or location block the prompt names, then the specific prompt.
2. Append **Negative Block**, in the negative field if the tool has one.
3. Generate several, and keep a reference image of each approved character and location to feed back in, so the look stays consistent.
4. Expect garbled text. Add real titles and labels later as live UI, never baked into the art. All game text is live HTML.

**Spoilers.** Sections 7, 8 and 9 must not show the Veil reveal. Sequences C to F in section 10 are reveal material, for internal use until you decide otherwise.

### Negative Block

```
No text, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no signatures, no UI. No real flags, real national insignia, real military unit patches, real company logos, real firearm brands or maker marks. No recognisable real person. No daylight, no sunshine, no blue sky, no golden hour, no neon cyberpunk, no fantasy or sci-fi armour, no gore, no blood, no wounded people, no captives. No cartoon or video game render look, no plastic skin, no extra fingers, no warped weapons.
```
